Refund Policy

Refunds: clear conditions, no fine print.

Whatss is the official WhatsApp Business API for businesses in Mozambique and Brazil. This policy defines when and how paid amounts can be refunded.

Last updated: August 10, 2026

⚡ Quick summary

Duplicate or mistaken charges are always fully refunded. The implementation fee is refundable if you cancel before work begins. Monthly fees and conversation credits are not refundable after activation, except in cases of ongoing service failure attributable to Whatss.

1 What services this applies to

This policy covers all payments made to HLCES for Whatss services: implementation of the official WhatsApp Business API, monthly management and support fees, and conversation credits (conversations served through the API).

2 Implementation fee (setup)

  • Paid once, before the setup of your account begins.
  • Fully refundable if you cancel before implementation work starts.
  • Not refundable after implementation begins, as it involves technical work already performed (account setup, verification, integration).

3 Monthly fees

  • Charged in advance, per monthly cycle, at the start of each period.
  • Not refundable proportionally mid-cycle: cancellation takes effect at the end of the current cycle and no further charges are made from then on.
  • If the account is suspended due to payment failure, reactivation may incur an administrative fee, communicated before charging.

4 Conversation credits

  • Credits purchased for serving conversations are consumable digital goods and are not refundable after activation on the account.
  • Exception: credits activated by mistake, duplicated or resulting from a billing error are fully refunded, as credit or refund, your choice.
  • Unused credits can be transferred to another Whatss account at your request, free of charge.

5 Cases with and without refund rights

SituationOutcome
Ongoing service failure attributable to Whatss (more than 72h without resolution) Proportional refund for the affected period Refundable
Duplicate or incorrect charge Full refund of the wrong amount Refundable
Cancellation before implementation starts Full refund of the setup fee Refundable
Monthly fee after the cycle starts (voluntary cancellation) No refund — service available until the end of the cycle Not refundable
Already activated conversation credits No refund — consumable digital good Not refundable
Dissatisfaction with Meta/WhatsApp features (outside our control) Reviewed case by case Case by case

6 How to request a refund

  • Send an email to info@hlces.com with the subject "Refund Request".
  • Include: name, registered WhatsApp number, description of the issue, payment date and receipt (if available).
  • We reply to all requests within 7 business days.

7 Processing and deadlines

  • Approved requests are returned via the same payment method used for the purchase.
  • The amount is credited in up to 10 business days after approval.
  • In cases where credit adjustment applies, the amount is credited to the Whatss account in up to 3 business days.

8 Still have questions?

Talk to us before deciding — we resolve most cases without bureaucracy.
